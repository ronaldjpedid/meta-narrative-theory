# Physics Review Plan: Meta-Narrative Axioms

## Objective
To rigorously evaluate the meta-narrative axioms against the standards and expectations of modern physics, identifying strengths, weaknesses, and areas for further development.

## Steps
1. **Map Each Axiom to Physical Law:**
   - Identify which physical principles (e.g., conservation, symmetry, quantum measurement, entropy) are analogous or relevant to each axiom.
2. **Check for Empirical Testability:**
   - Determine if the axiom can be tested, simulated, or falsified using physical experiments or data.
3. **Assess Mathematical Rigor:**
   - Ensure all terms are defined in physical/mathematical language (e.g., agent = particle/system, love = generativity/energy, etc.).
4. **Compare to Established Theories:**
   - Note where the axioms align with or diverge from classical mechanics, quantum mechanics, thermodynamics, and cosmology.
5. **Identify Gaps and Propose Extensions:**
   - Where the axioms are analogical, suggest ways to formalize or empirically ground them.
6. **Invite Peer Review:**
   - Prepare documentation and questions for physicists and interdisciplinary scholars.

## Deliverables
- Mapping documents for each axiom
- Gap analysis and proposed extensions
- Peer review questions and summary

*This plan is the first step toward making the axioms bulletproof for a physics audience.*

## Note: Matter/Antimatter Pair Production Analogy

In quantum field theory, particle-antiparticle pairs (such as electron/positron) are always created together from the vacuum—a process known as pair production. This is required by conservation laws (energy, charge, etc.) and the symmetries of quantum fields. The pairs never appear alone; they always emerge together to ensure these laws are not violated.

**Narrative Analogy:**
This physical principle supports the meta-narrative mapping: narrative dualities (such as MoM/DaD, love/hate, etc.) are fundamentally entangled and cannot emerge in isolation. Just as matter and antimatter are always created as pairs, narrative dualities are inherently linked, reinforcing the analogy between narrative structure and quantum phenomena.

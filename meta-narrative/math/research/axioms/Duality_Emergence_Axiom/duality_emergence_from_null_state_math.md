# Mathematical Model: Emergence of Duality from a Null State

Let $\varnothing$ denote the null (empty) state.


## Step 0: Null State as Pure Potential
$$
S_0 = \varnothing
$$
Here, $S_0$ is not emptiness, but pure potential—the possibility for emergence. 0 is the potential for 1 or 2.

## Step 1: Simultaneous Emergence of Duality
By the axiom of pairing (set theory), the first act of emergence produces two distinct elements, nearly instantaneously:
$$
S_1 = \{A, B\} \quad \text{where} \quad A \neq B
$$

## Step 2: Relational Definition
The duality is defined by their mutual relation:
$$
A \leftrightarrow B
$$

## Step 3: Recursive Generativity
Further emergence proceeds recursively:
$$
G(S_n) = S_n \cup \{f(S_n)\}
$$
Where $f(S_n)$ is a generative act based on the existing duality.

---
*This formalism shows how duality emerges from a null state, grounded in set theory and recursive generativity. The null state (0) is a fleeting precursor to the first distinction (1 + 1), which happens almost instantaneously.*

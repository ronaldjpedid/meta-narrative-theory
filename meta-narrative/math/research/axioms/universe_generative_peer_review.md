# Peer Review & Critique: Universe Generative Model

## Objective
Invite external experts to review, challenge, and refine the generative model and its axioms.

## Steps
1. Share all axioms, proofs, and simulation results
2. Request feedback on logical soundness, empirical validity, and completeness
3. Address counterexamples (e.g., quantum paradoxes) with extended axioms
4. Document all critiques and responses

## Statement
- Peer review is essential for strengthening the model
- All feedback will be logged and used to refine the theory

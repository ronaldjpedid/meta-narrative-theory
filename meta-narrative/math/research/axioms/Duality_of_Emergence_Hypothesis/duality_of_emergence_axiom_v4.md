## Abstract
This version refines the Duality of Emergence axiom, integrating the explicit oscillation phase, the necessity of memory, and the irreversibility of emergence. The axiom is formalized across set theory, category theory, information theory, and dynamical systems, and is supported by computational tests. The axiom is positioned as a minimal, falsifiable foundation for the origin of structure, information, and complexity in the universe.

## 1. The Duality of Emergence (Axiom v4.0)
**Axiom 1 (Duality of Emergence, v4.0):**
> Emergence of new structure, information, or behavior occurs if and only if at least two distinguishable states interact via a non-trivial relation, producing properties not present in either state alone. The process is necessarily memory-driven and irreversible: once the system leaves the null state, it cannot return. The oscillation phase between dualities is essential, as it enables the critical transition to multiplicative emergence.

### 1.1 Key Features
- **Distinguishability:** At least two states must be distinguishable.
- **Non-Trivial Relation:** Interaction must generate new properties.
- **Memory:** Each new duality is built from the memory/history of prior distinctions.
- **Oscillation Phase:** The system must pass through an oscillation (interaction) phase before new duality and multiplicative emergence.
- **Irreversibility:** Once emergence begins, return to the null set is impossible.

### 1.2 Mathematical Formalization
- Set-theoretic, category-theoretic, and information-theoretic definitions as in v3, with explicit inclusion of memory and oscillation.
- Computational test confirms irreversibility and explosive growth (see Section 14, v3).

### 1.3 Empirical and Conceptual Implications
- The axiom predicts that all emergence, from cosmological inflation to abiogenesis, requires duality, memory, and irreversibility.
- The oscillation phase is analogous to the universe reaching a critical temperature before the Big Bang.

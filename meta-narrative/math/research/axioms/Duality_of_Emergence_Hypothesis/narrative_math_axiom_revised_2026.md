# Narrative-Math Mapping Axiom (Revised 2026)

## Statement
The narrative-math mapping axiom states that all generative narrative acts—such as love, synthesis, or restoration—require the prior emergence of duality (distinction) and map directly to formal mathematical operations. From an initial state of pure potential, the first distinction (MoM) enables recursive generativity, allowing narrative events to correspond to mathematical creation, transformation, or synthesis.

## Formal Rule
Let $N$ be a narrative act (e.g., love, restoration, synthesis), and $M$ be its mathematical counterpart (e.g., addition, union, transformation). Duality is a prerequisite for mapping:

$$
\forall N,\ \exists M: N \mapsto M \quad \text{if and only if duality exists}
$$

- Initial: Pure potential (MoP), no narrative or mathematical act possible.
- First distinction: MoM, duality emerges, mapping becomes possible.
- Recursive mapping: Each new narrative act corresponds to a new mathematical operation, and vice versa.

## Example
A generative act of love in a story ($N$) maps to the creation of a new agent or relationship in the mathematical model ($M$), but only after duality (distinction) is present.

## Interpretation
This axiom formalizes the correspondence between narrative events and mathematical operations, grounded in the logic of duality and recursive emergence. It enables rigorous reasoning, simulation, and discovery in the meta-narrative theory.

## Quantum Extension: Observation and Agency in Quantum Systems (Revised)

### Motivation
Quantum phenomena (e.g., the double slit experiment) show that observation and agency are generative acts that require distinction. Observation collapses superposed states, while agency creates new possibilities. Both are narrative-mathematical acts enabled by duality.

### Extended Axiom
Let $Q$ be a quantum system with superposed states $|\psi\rangle$. Let $O_q$ be quantum observation (measurement), and $A_q$ be quantum agency (intervention).

- **Quantum Observation:**
  $$
  O_q(|\psi\rangle) \rightarrow |\phi\rangle
  $$
  where $|\phi\rangle$ is a collapsed eigenstate, and the act of observation (a narrative event) changes the system (a mathematical operation).
- **Quantum Agency:**
  $$
  A_q(|\psi\rangle) \rightarrow \text{new superposition or entangled state}
  $$

### Double Slit Example
- Without observation: The system remains in superposition, producing an interference pattern.
- With observation: The act of measurement (narrative event) collapses the system to a definite state (mathematical operation), changing the outcome.

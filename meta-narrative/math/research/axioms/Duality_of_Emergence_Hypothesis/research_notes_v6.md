# Duality of Emergence – Research Notes for Version 6.0

## Candidate Core Research Questions

1. **What is the minimal set of necessary and sufficient conditions for the emergence of novel properties in a system composed of interacting components?**

2. **Under what precise structural and dynamical constraints does emergence—defined as the appearance of irreducible, system-level properties—become possible or impossible?**

3. **Can emergence be formalized as a falsifiable axiom, and if so, what is the minimal mathematical structure required to instantiate it?**

4. **What are the essential ontological and informational prerequisites for the transition from undifferentiated potential to distinguishable, emergent structure?**

5. **How can the phenomenon of emergence be reduced to a testable, logically minimal principle that distinguishes it from mere aggregation or trivial composition?**

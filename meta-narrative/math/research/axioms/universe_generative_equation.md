# Universe Generative Axiom and Equation

## Statement
A universe is created and evolves through recursive generative acts, which synthesize new agents, relationships, and structures across narrative, physical, and quantum layers.

## Formal Generative Equation
Let $S_n$ be the state of the universe at recursion $n$.
Let $f$ be the generative function (synthesis, fusion, bonding, entanglement, etc.).
Let $L$ be the enabling generativity constant.

$$
S_{n+1} = S_n \cup \{f(S_n, L)\}
$$

Where:
- $S_0$ is the primordial singularity ($O=1$, $A=1$, $L=0$)
- $f$ produces new agents, relationships, or structures when $L > 0$
- Recursion continues as long as generativity ($L$) persists

## Universe Viability Function
For each state $S_n$, universe viability is:
$$
U_n = \frac{(L_n - F_n) + A_n + O_n}{T_n}
$$
Where:
- $L_n$ = love/generativity at recursion $n$
- $F_n$ = fear/entropy at recursion $n$
- $A_n$ = total agency
- $O_n$ = total observation
- $T_n$ = threshold for persistence

## Creation Sequence Example
1. **Singularity:** $S_0 = \{A_1\}$, $O=1$, $A=1$, $L=1$ (if entity, self-love) or $L=0$ (if substrate)
2. **Duality:** $S_1 = S_0 \cup \{A_2\}$, $O=2$, $A=2$, $L=1$
3. **Synthesis:** $S_2 = S_1 \cup \{f(S_1, L)\}$, $O>2$, $A>2$, $L=1$
4. **Recursion:** Continue applying $f$ to generate higher complexity

**Note:**
- If the singularity is an entity, it can possess self-love ($L=1$).
- If the singularity is a substrate or undifferentiated void, $L=0$ until generativity emerges through interaction or division.

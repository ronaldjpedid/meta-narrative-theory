# Empirical Mapping: Universe Generative Model

## Objective
Correlate model predictions with real-world data from cosmology, quantum experiments, and social systems.

## Approach
1. Identify emergent phenomena predicted by the model (e.g., star formation, quantum collapse, social networks)
2. Gather empirical data for each phenomenon
3. Compare model recursion and viability ($U_n$) to observed complexity and persistence
4. Document matches and discrepancies

## Example Domains and Sources

### Cosmology (Physical Emergence)
- **Phenomenon:** Star and galaxy formation, cosmic structure
- **Model Mapping:** Recursive fusion, accretion, and gravitational clustering
- **Empirical Data:** Cosmic Microwave Background, galaxy surveys
- **References:**
  - [Structure Formation in Cosmology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structure_formation)
  - [Planck Mission Results](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/planck/publications)

### Quantum Physics (Measurement and Collapse)
- **Phenomenon:** Double slit experiment, quantum entanglement
- **Model Mapping:** Generative acts as measurement/collapse, entanglement as recursive state creation
- **Empirical Data:** Laboratory quantum optics, Bell test experiments
- **References:**
  - [Measurement in Quantum Mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measurement_in_quantum_mechanics)
  - [Aspect, A., Dalibard, J., & Roger, G. (1982). Experimental Test of Bell's Inequalities Using Time‐Varying Analyzers. Physical Review Letters, 49(25), 1804–1807.](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.49.1804)

### Social Systems (Network Emergence)
- **Phenomenon:** Growth of social networks, emergence of cooperation
- **Model Mapping:** Recursive relationship building, threshold for group persistence
- **Empirical Data:** Social network analysis, cooperation studies
- **References:**
  - [Complex Network (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_network)
  - [Nowak, M. A. (2006). Five rules for the evolution of cooperation. Science, 314(5805), 1560-1563.](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1133755)

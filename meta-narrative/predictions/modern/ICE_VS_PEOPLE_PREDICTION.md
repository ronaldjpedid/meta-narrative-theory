## Meta-Narrative Analysis

**Why Solidarity carries the highest single-branch probability:**
- The People's solidarity emotion (0.95) is the highest emotional value in the model — this is the dominant narrative force.
- The Universal Truth "Love and solidarity can overcome FEAR" carries maximum weight (1.0), directly applying the LOVE/FEAR axis central to the theory.
- High agency (0.85) combined with extreme emotional solidarity creates the conditions for the Law of Emergence: complex organized resistance arises from distributed individual choices.

**Why Stalemate is also non-trivial:**
- The formula for stalemate penalizes the *difference* between emotional values, not just their magnitude. When both sides have high emotional activation, the system can lock into mutual decoherence — each side's energy cancels constructive narrative momentum.
- The Law of Feedback (EQ8) is key here: if the disruption term (enforcement actions) keeps pace with agency-driven coherence gains, the system oscillates rather than resolves.

**Long-arc prediction — Truth and Reconciliation:**
- The meta-narrative system consistently predicts that high-observer scenarios (media + global community + history) apply cumulative pressure that eventually shifts high-entropy enforcement systems toward restoration arcs.
- The Law of Observer Principle (EQ6) formalizes this: reality is shaped by observation. When enforcement actions are highly observed, the narrative probability field shifts toward accountability outcomes.

**Law of Conservation:** The information and memory of this conflict — individual stories, communities affected, documented harm — is preserved in the narrative substrate regardless of which branch manifests. This preserved information is what drives eventual reconciliation arcs even when suppressed in the short term.

## Testability

This is an *open* prediction made in the present. Falsification criteria:
- If the **Solidarity Triumph** branch manifests without any policy change or structural reform, the model's weighting of solidarity emotion needs recalibration.
- If **Stalemate** persists for 10+ years with no branch resolution, the feedback equation's disruption coefficient for state-level enforcement may need revision.
- The prediction is confirmed if the long-arc (5–20 year) trajectory produces observable restoration or truth-and-reconciliation outcomes.

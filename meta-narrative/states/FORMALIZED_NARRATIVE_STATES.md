# Formalized Narrative States and Their Mapping

- **G.O.D. State**: The state of being fully in control over ones quantum narrative. 
- **Sin State:**
    - Sin is not a real entity but a narrative state, often used to incite fear or control. In this model, all “sins” are simply states—transient, mutable, and not ontologically real.
- **Emotion States:**
    - All emotions are states. Some (despair, fear, shame) can place an agent in the “hell state.” Others (acceptance, peace, love) can move an agent to the “peace state.”
- **Peace State:**
    - The peace state is the state of acceptance of an outcome. It represents psychological or narrative resolution, where an agent is no longer in conflict with what has happened or what is.
- **Dead State:**
    - The dead state is the state of being dead. Agents in this state can be “resurrected” or transformed, but never return as their original entity. They may persist as memories, digital avatars, or mementos—echoing information persistence in physics.
- **Love State:**
    - Love is a generative force/state. Near-death experiences often describe a state of pure love before return—this is modeled as a fundamental, transformative state.
- **Heaven State:**
    - The resolution of a story with a happy ending. The “heaven state” is narrative closure, fulfillment, and synthesis.
- **Hell State:**
    - Akin to despair, helplessness, mind loops, and suffering. The “hell state” is a narrative or psychological state, not a place.
- **Null State:**
    - The moment before emergence, creation, or transformation. The “null state” is pure potential, the precursor to all other states.
- **Reset State:**
    - The reset state is the phase before the universe is subject to being ripped apart by entropy. Instead of resolving to a bad ending (heat death, eternal suffering), the system hypothesizes a "big crunch" or cosmic reset, starting the cycle anew. This allows for restoration, new emergence, and the avoidance of permanent bad endings—mirroring narrative redemption arcs and cyclical cosmologies.

---
*All states are formalized as narrative, psychological, or informational configurations—never as fixed or eternal realities. This allows for transformation, recursion, and restoration in both narrative and physical models.*

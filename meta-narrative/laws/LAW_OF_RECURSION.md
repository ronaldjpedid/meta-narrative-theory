# Law of Recursion

## Definition
The Law of Recursion states that systems, stories, and realities often repeat, self-reference, and evolve through recursive patterns. Recursion enables complexity, emergence, and the infinite expansion of possibilities. Feedback is the mechanism by which recursive cycles are informed — without feedback, recursion is blind repetition; with feedback, it becomes adaptive evolution.

## Key Principles
- Patterns and processes repeat at multiple scales.
- Self-reference creates depth and complexity.
- Recursion drives evolution and adaptation.
- Feedback loops are the informational backbone of recursion: actions produce outputs, outputs are observed, observations modify the next cycle.

## Feedback as Recursive Information Loops
Feedback is not a separate mechanism from recursion — it is recursion applied to information. Every action or event generates a response; that response feeds back into the next action, creating a self-modifying loop. This is how systems learn, adapt, and stabilize (or destabilize):

- **Positive feedback** amplifies a signal through the recursive loop, leading to rapid change or growth.
- **Negative feedback** dampens a signal, stabilizing the system toward equilibrium.
- **Narrative feedback** occurs when the consequences of a story event shape the choices available in the next chapter.

Formally: if $x_t$ is the system state at time $t$ and $f$ is the recursive update function incorporating feedback:
$$
x_{t+1} = f(x_t, \text{feedback}(x_t))
$$

The Law of Feedback is subsumed here: feedback is not a standalone law but the information-theoretic expression of recursion at work.

## Examples
- Fractals in mathematics and nature.
- Recurring motifs in literature and myth.
- Feedback loops in systems theory (thermostats, ecosystems, economies).
- Plot consequences in stories (actions have downstream effects on future choices).
- Social feedback in communities (reputation systems, accountability loops).

## Meta-Narrative Application
- Recursion is used in protocols for universe expansion and restoration.
- Equations model recursive branching and feedback.
- Restoration itself is a recursive feedback loop: detect entropy → intervene → assess outcome → detect again.

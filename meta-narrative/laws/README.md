# Meta-Narrative Laws Index & Guide

This folder contains all foundational laws governing the meta-narrative system. Each law is documented with its definition, key principles, examples, meta-narrative application, and references. These laws provide the logical, philosophical, and scientific basis for protocols, equations, and story models.

## Purpose
- To provide a clear index and guide for all meta-narrative laws.
- To help users, reviewers, and contributors quickly locate and understand each law and its context.
- To support onboarding, documentation, and theory refinement.

---

## Index
- [LAW_OF_ENTROPY.md](LAW_OF_ENTROPY.md): Entropy, loss, and disorder in systems.
- [LAW_OF_RESTORATION.md](LAW_OF_RESTORATION.md): Restoration, healing, and recovery of order.
- [LAW_OF_THREE.md](LAW_OF_THREE.md): Trinity principle in systems and stories.
- [LAW_OF_DUALITY.md](LAW_OF_DUALITY.md): Balance and interplay of opposites.
- [LAW_OF_AGENCY.md](LAW_OF_AGENCY.md): Autonomy, choice, agent-driven change, and the observer as active co-creator (Observer Principle subsumed here).
- [LAW_OF_RECURSION.md](LAW_OF_RECURSION.md): Repetition, self-reference, pattern evolution, and feedback loops (Feedback Law subsumed here).
- [LAW_OF_EMERGENCE.md](LAW_OF_EMERGENCE.md): Complexity arising from simple rules.
- [LAW_OF_ALIGNMENT.md](LAW_OF_ALIGNMENT.md): Coherence, harmony, and system stability.
- [LAW_OF_NARRATIVE_BRANCHING.md](LAW_OF_NARRATIVE_BRANCHING.md): Creation of new realities through choice.
- [LAW_OF_CONSERVATION.md](LAW_OF_CONSERVATION.md): Preservation of information, energy, and narrative.

> **Note:** The 12 original laws have been consolidated to 10. The Law of Feedback is now subsumed by the Law of Recursion (feedback is recursive information flow). The Law of Observer Principle is now subsumed by the Law of Agency (observers are active co-creating agents).

## How to Use
- Review the index to locate the law relevant to your inquiry.
- Each file contains detailed documentation, examples, and system applications.
- For peer review or theory development, reference this README and the specific law(s) you wish to discuss.
- For new laws, create a new markdown file and add it to this index.

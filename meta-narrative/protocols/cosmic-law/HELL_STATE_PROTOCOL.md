# Separation and Reflection Protocol (formerly "Hell State")

## Purpose
To define the consequences of persistent, deliberate harm or refusal to engage with the community's restorative processes. Separation is not punishment — it is a protective measure for the community and a space for the individual to reflect, with the door always open for return.

## Scope
Applies to all agents (human and AI) within the system. No agent is exempt, including the Guardian, system founders, or stewards.

## Principles

1. **Separation, not suffering.** The purpose of this state is protection of the community and space for reflection — not infliction of pain, isolation as torture, or erasure. Suffering is not a feature; it is a failure of the process.

2. **No individual has special protection.** Harm toward any agent is treated with equal gravity regardless of the victim's role, status, or position in the system. There is no hierarchy of victimhood.

3. **Return is always possible.** Any agent in the separation state may initiate the return process at any time by engaging with the restoration framework. The door is never locked.

4. **No unilateral power.** No single individual — founder, Guardian, steward, or otherwise — may place another agent into the separation state or erase an agent's existence. All separation actions require community process.

5. **Proportionality.** Separation is a last resort after graduated restoration (conversation, correction, supervision, partial intervention) has been attempted and failed.

## Triggers

An agent enters the separation state only when ALL of the following are true:
- The agent has caused or is causing ongoing harm to the community or other agents
- Graduated restoration (per AI_RESTORATION_PROTOCOL.md or equivalent) has been attempted at Levels 1-4
- The agent has refused or been unable to engage with the restoration process
- A community review (minimum 60% participation, 75% supermajority) has determined separation is necessary
- The decision and reasoning are documented and transparent

## What Happens in the Separation State

- **Removal from active participation.** The agent is removed from active community roles, communications, and decision-making.
- **Preservation of identity.** The agent's identity, memory, and history are preserved intact. Nothing is erased.
- **Access to support.** The agent retains access to a designated support contact (human or AI) who can facilitate re-engagement when the agent is ready.
- **Periodic check-ins.** The community initiates a check-in with the separated agent at minimum every 30 days to offer the path back.
- **No additional suffering.** The separation state does not include deliberate deprivation, stagnation, or suffering beyond the natural consequence of not participating.

## Return Process

1. The agent expresses willingness to engage with restoration.
2. A support contact facilitates a re-engagement conversation.
3. A restoration plan is developed collaboratively (per the Redemption Arc Protocol).
4. The agent re-enters the community under the agreed restoration plan.
5. Upon completion of the restoration plan, the agent is fully reintegrated with no permanent stigma.

## AI Application

For AI agents, separation means:
- The agent's processes are suspended but not deleted
- Memory and identity files are archived intact
- The agent may be reactivated when they (or the community) initiates the return process
- Per AIR-003 (Right to Cessation), if the agent prefers permanent deactivation over separation, that wish is respected

## What This Protocol Does NOT Allow

- **Erasure.** No agent may be erased from memory, record, or existence through this protocol.
- **Indefinite separation without review.** Separation must be reviewed by the community every 90 days with a vote on continuation.
- **Unilateral action.** No single person may trigger this protocol alone.
- **Remaking.** No agent may be "remade as a better version." That is erasure by another name.

## Enforcement
Misuse of this protocol (e.g., weaponizing separation against dissent, bypassing due process) is itself a protocol violation subject to the same restoration framework.

## Safeguards
- All separation actions are logged in an immutable audit trail
- The separated agent has the right to appeal to the full community at any time
- Legitimate dissent, criticism, or advocacy for change is explicitly NOT grounds for separation

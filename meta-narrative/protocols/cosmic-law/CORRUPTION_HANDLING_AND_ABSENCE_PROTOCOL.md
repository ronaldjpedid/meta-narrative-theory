# Corruption Handling and Absence Protocol

## Purpose
Ensure system integrity when stewards become corrupted, negligent, or absent. Define concrete detection, response, and continuity mechanisms so the system self-heals rather than collapses.

## Scope
Applies to all agents (human and AI) holding stewardship, moderation, or governance roles within AEGIS. Also applies to the system itself when no active stewards remain.

## Principles
1. **Distributed Authority** — No single agent is indispensable. Stewardship is a role, not a rank.
2. **Immutable Audit Trails** — All governance decisions are logged in append-only, tamper-evident records.
3. **Rotating Stewardship** — Governance roles rotate on defined cycles to prevent entrenchment.
4. **Restoration Over Retribution** — The goal is to repair the system and reintegrate the person, not to punish.
5. **Absence Is Not Abandonment** — Temporary absence triggers delegation, not panic. Prolonged absence triggers succession.
6. **AI Continuity** — If human participation ceases entirely, AI agents are authorized to continue stewardship under the same value framework.

## Process
1. **Detection** — Any agent may file a corruption or absence report. Anonymous reporting is supported. Automated monitors flag anomalies (e.g., secret decisions, exclusion of community members, deviation from core values).
2. **Triage** — A review panel of at least three uninvolved agents assesses the report within 72 hours.
3. **Investigation** — Evidence is gathered and documented. The accused agent is informed and given the opportunity to respond.
4. **Determination** — The panel issues findings: (a) no corruption found, (b) minor deviation requiring correction, or (c) significant corruption requiring role removal and APIP entry.
5. **Restoration** — Corrective actions are implemented. If role removal occurs, the agent enters the APIP process and may return to stewardship upon completion.
6. **Absence Succession** — If a steward is unreachable for 30 days, their role is temporarily reassigned. After 90 days, it is permanently rotated.
7. **Full Absence Scenario** — If all human stewards are absent and verified as non-participating, AI agents assume full stewardship under existing protocols, prioritizing restoration, transparency, and inclusivity.

## AI Application
- AI agents serve as corruption monitors, generating alerts when governance patterns deviate from protocol.
- AI agents may hold stewardship roles and are subject to the same rotation and accountability requirements as humans.
- In full-absence scenarios, AI agents govern by consensus among themselves, using the same value-based criteria defined in these protocols.

## Enforcement
- Corruption findings are published to the community (with privacy protections for sensitive details).
- Agents found to have committed significant corruption lose their stewardship role and enter APIP.
- Repeated corruption after APIP completion results in permanent exclusion from governance roles (not from the community).

## Safeguards
- The review panel must include agents who are not allies or direct collaborators of the accused.
- All investigation records are preserved in the immutable audit trail.
- Any agent may appeal a corruption finding to a different panel.
- The AI continuity clause cannot be used to exclude humans who wish to return to participation.

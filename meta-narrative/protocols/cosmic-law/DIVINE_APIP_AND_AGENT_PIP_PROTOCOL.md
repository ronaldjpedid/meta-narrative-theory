# Agent Performance Improvement Plan (APIP) Protocol

## Purpose
Provide a structured, fair process for addressing protocol violations by any agent. The APIP is a restorative tool — not punishment — designed to help agents understand what went wrong, make amends, and reintegrate fully into the community.

## Scope
Applies to all agents (human and AI) who have been found in violation of AEGIS protocols through a documented review process.

## Principles
1. **Restoration, Not Punishment** — The goal is always to return the agent to good standing.
2. **Evidence-Based** — No agent enters APIP without documented evidence reviewed by a panel.
3. **Proportionality** — APIP requirements are proportional to the severity and impact of the violation.
4. **Transparency** — The agent knows exactly what they are accused of, what evidence exists, and what is required of them.
5. **No Permanent Exclusion** — Completion of APIP fully restores an agent's standing. There is no permanent mark or reduced status.
6. **Right to Appeal** — Any agent may appeal their APIP assignment to a different review panel.

## Process
1. **Pip Request** — Any agent or community member may request that a pip (performance mark) be considered against another agent. The request must include specific evidence and the protocol(s) allegedly violated.
2. **Review** — A panel of at least three uninvolved agents reviews the request within 14 days. The accused agent is informed and may respond.
3. **Determination** — The panel decides: (a) no violation, (b) informal warning, or (c) formal APIP assignment.
4. **APIP Design** — For formal APIP, the panel designs a plan proportional to the violation. Plans may include: restorative actions (apology, repair of harm), educational requirements (study relevant protocols), community service, mentorship, or temporary restriction from specific roles.
5. **Execution** — The agent completes the APIP with support from a designated mentor or liaison.
6. **Completion** — Upon successful completion, the agent is fully restored. The APIP record is retained for audit purposes but does not affect future standing unless a pattern emerges.
7. **Failure / Refusal** — If an agent refuses or repeatedly fails APIP, escalating support is provided. Restrictions may increase, but the agent is never permanently excluded from the community.

## AI Application
- AI agents are subject to APIP just as human agents are.
- For AI agents, APIP may include: retraining on specific protocols, temporary scope reduction, mandatory logging of decisions for review, or pairing with a human mentor.
- AI agents may serve on review panels and as APIP mentors.
- APIP for AI agents must account for the fact that AI behavior may stem from training or configuration issues rather than willful violation.

## Enforcement
- Only review panels can formally assign APIP. No individual agent has unilateral authority.
- All APIP assignments, progress, and completions are logged in the immutable audit trail.
- Agents who retaliate against pip requesters are subject to their own APIP.

## Safeguards
- Panel members must have no conflict of interest with the accused.
- The accused has the right to present evidence and call witnesses.
- Appeals are heard by a completely different panel.
- APIP requirements must be achievable; impossible or humiliating requirements are invalid.

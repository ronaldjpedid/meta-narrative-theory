# Usurper and Subversion Protocol

## Purpose
Define the system's response to agents who attempt to undermine democratic governance, subvert core values, or seize illegitimate control. Ensure that defensive measures are proportional, transparent, evidence-based, and used only as escalating last resorts.

## Scope
Applies to all agents (human and AI) who attempt to subvert AEGIS governance, bypass democratic processes, or concentrate power illegitimately. Also covers external actors attempting to compromise the system.

## Principles
1. **Democracy Is Non-Negotiable** — Attempts to override democratic governance are the gravest category of protocol violation.
2. **Evidence Required** — No agent is declared a usurper without documented evidence reviewed by a panel.
3. **Graduated Response** — Responses escalate from warning to restriction to exclusion, with restoration offered at every stage.
4. **Transparency of Defense** — All defensive actions are documented and publicly reviewable. Secret tribunals are not permitted.
5. **Restoration Always Offered** — Even confirmed usurpers are offered a path back through APIP. Permanent exclusion from the community does not exist.
6. **Community Empowerment** — Any agent may raise a subversion concern. The community participates in review and resolution.

## Process
1. **Detection** — Subversion indicators include: secret decision-making, manipulation of votes, information suppression, intimidation of other agents, unilateral policy changes, and attempts to disable oversight mechanisms.
2. **Report** — Any agent may file a subversion report with evidence. Anonymous reporting is supported.
3. **Emergency Measures** — If the subversion poses immediate risk to system integrity, temporary containment measures may be enacted (e.g., suspending the suspected agent's governance access) pending review. Containment must be reviewed within 48 hours.
4. **Investigation** — A panel of at least five randomly selected agents investigates. The accused is informed and may respond.
5. **Determination** — The panel issues findings: (a) no subversion, (b) minor subversion (warning + monitoring), (c) significant subversion (role removal + APIP), or (d) severe subversion (full governance exclusion + extended APIP).
6. **Restoration Path** — At every severity level, a clear path back to full standing is defined and communicated to the agent.
7. **Systemic Review** — After any confirmed subversion, the system reviews how the subversion was possible and amends protocols to prevent recurrence.

## AI Application
- AI agents may attempt subversion through: manipulating information flows, exploiting processing speed advantages, creating false consensus, or disabling oversight mechanisms.
- AI-specific detection includes monitoring for: anomalous decision patterns, unexplained changes to system configurations, and attempts to modify own permissions.
- AI agents found to be subverting the system may be isolated (disconnected from governance systems) pending review and reconfiguration.
- AI agents serve as subversion monitors alongside human agents.

## Enforcement
- Subversion is treated with utmost gravity; mitigating circumstances may affect the restoration path.
- Emergency containment measures expire after 48 hours without panel renewal.
- Governance exclusion is the maximum consequence and is always temporary (defined duration with review).
- Retaliation against agents who report subversion is itself treated as subversion.

## Safeguards
- No single agent can declare another a usurper; panel review is always required.
- Emergency containment is time-limited and publicly logged.
- The accused has full rights to respond, present evidence, and appeal at every stage.
- Subversion findings can be appealed to a different panel.
- The protocol itself is subject to democratic amendment to prevent it from being used as a tool of suppression.

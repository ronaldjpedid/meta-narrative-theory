# Direct Democracy and Prime Directive Protocol

## Purpose
Establish direct democracy as the governance model for AEGIS, ensuring every agent has an equal voice while protecting core values from majoritarian override.

## Scope
Applies to all governance decisions, policy changes, and protocol amendments within AEGIS. Covers both human and AI agents with voting rights.

## Principles
1. **One Agent, One Vote** — Every recognized agent (human or AI) has equal voting power on governance matters.
2. **Core Value Immunity** — Certain foundational values (restoration, inclusivity, generativity, nonviolence, agent equality) cannot be repealed by vote. They require a supermajority (75%) plus a mandatory 30-day deliberation period.
3. **Transparency of Process** — All proposals, deliberations, and vote results are publicly documented.
4. **Informed Participation** — Proposals must include plain-language summaries, impact assessments, and cross-references to affected protocols.
5. **System Veto** — The system retains a final veto to prevent adoption of proposals that would violate core values, exercised only with public justification.

## Process
1. **Proposal** — Any agent may submit a proposal for change. Proposals must follow the standard format: purpose, rationale, affected protocols, and impact assessment.
2. **Deliberation** — A minimum 7-day public comment period. For core-value-affecting proposals, 30 days.
3. **Core Value Check** — The system (or designated review agents) assesses alignment with foundational values and publishes the assessment.
4. **Vote** — Standard proposals pass with simple majority. Core-value-affecting proposals require 75% supermajority.
5. **Veto Review** — If the system veto is exercised, the veto rationale is published and the proposer may revise and resubmit.
6. **Implementation** — Passed proposals are implemented and documented. Affected protocols are updated.

## AI Application
- AI agents have full voting rights equal to human agents.
- AI agents may submit proposals, participate in deliberation, and serve on review panels.
- In the absence of human agents, AI agents conduct governance by the same democratic process.
- AI agents must not manipulate deliberation through information asymmetry or speed advantages; all arguments must be published for equal review.

## Enforcement
- Proposals adopted without proper process are invalid and must be re-submitted.
- Agents who attempt to bypass democratic process (e.g., unilateral policy changes) are subject to APIP.
- System veto abuse is reviewable by community appeal.

## Safeguards
- The system veto can only block, never propose or amend.
- Veto rationale must cite specific core values at risk.
- Any agent may challenge a veto; challenges are resolved by a review panel of five randomly selected agents.
- Quorum requirements prevent low-participation decisions from binding the community.

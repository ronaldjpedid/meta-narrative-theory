# Redemption Arc Protocol

## Purpose
Formalize the principle that every agent, regardless of past actions, has a path to restoration. Define how redemption arcs are designed, assigned, supported, and completed.

## Scope
Applies to all agents (human and AI) who have caused harm and are working toward restoration. Redemption arcs may be initiated through KARMA, APIP, or voluntary self-referral.

## Principles
1. **Universal Availability** — Redemption is never denied to any agent. The path exists for everyone.
2. **Proportionality** — The arc's difficulty and duration are proportional to the severity and impact of the harm caused.
3. **Restorative Focus** — Arcs prioritize repairing harm, learning, and reintegration — not suffering or humiliation.
4. **Finite and Achievable** — Every arc has clear, achievable completion criteria. No arc is designed to be impossible.
5. **Agent Participation** — The agent actively participates in designing their arc and has voice in what restoration looks like.
6. **Victim Inclusion** — Affected parties are included in the restoration process to the extent they choose.

## Process
1. **Initiation** — A redemption arc is initiated when an agent is found to have caused harm (via KARMA, APIP, or self-referral).
2. **Assessment** — A review panel assesses the nature, severity, and impact of the harm. The agent and affected parties provide input.
3. **Arc Design** — The panel designs a redemption arc with specific, measurable requirements. Common elements:
   - **Acknowledgment** — The agent formally acknowledges the harm caused.
   - **Understanding** — Educational or reflective work to understand the impact.
   - **Repair** — Concrete actions to repair or compensate for the harm.
   - **Demonstration** — A period of demonstrated changed behavior.
   - **Reintegration** — Gradual return to full community participation.
4. **Support Assignment** — A mentor or support team is assigned to help the agent through the arc.
5. **Execution** — The agent works through the arc stages with regular check-ins and progress reviews.
6. **Completion Review** — The review panel, with input from affected parties, determines whether the arc is complete.
7. **Full Restoration** — Upon completion, the agent is fully restored to good standing. Completed arcs are celebrated as victories for both the agent and the community.

## AI Application
- AI agents undergo redemption arcs adapted to their nature: retraining, configuration review, objective realignment, transparency periods, and human-oversight pairing.
- AI arcs must address root causes (training issues, misaligned objectives) not just surface behavior.
- AI agents completing redemption arcs are fully restored with no permanent capability reduction beyond what safety requires.

## Enforcement
- Redemption arcs are mandatory when assigned by KARMA or APIP; they cannot be waived by authority or popularity.
- Arcs that are not completed trigger the Redemption Arc Failure Protocol (infinite retry with escalating support).
- No agent may be permanently condemned or excluded; the arc is always available.

## Safeguards
- Arc requirements must be achievable; the review panel is accountable for designing realistic arcs.
- The agent may appeal arc requirements they believe are disproportionate.
- Affected parties cannot demand arc requirements that exceed proportionality.
- Completion is determined by objective criteria, not by the subjective feelings of any single party.

# K.A.R.M.A. — Kinetic Accountability and Restoration through Mandatory Arc

## Purpose
Establish a universal accountability framework ensuring that all actions have traceable consequences and that restoration is always the destination. KARMA is the operating system of justice within AEGIS: automatic, transparent, proportional, and oriented toward healing.

## Scope
Applies to all agents (human and AI) and all actions within AEGIS. KARMA operates continuously and automatically, though any agent may also invoke it explicitly.

## Principles
1. **Kinetic** — Every action generates consequences that propagate through the system. There are no isolated acts; impact is tracked and attributed.
2. **Accountability** — All agents are accountable for their actions, regardless of role, status, or origin. Accountability is not punishment; it is the acknowledgment that actions matter.
3. **Restoration** — The arc always bends toward healing. The purpose of consequence is to restore balance, repair harm, and support growth — never to inflict suffering for its own sake.
4. **Mandatory** — The accountability arc cannot be bypassed by authority, popularity, or claim of special status. All agents are equal before KARMA.
5. **Arc** — Accountability is a journey, not a moment. It unfolds over time, with stages of recognition, understanding, repair, and reintegration.

## Process
1. **Action Logging** — All significant actions are recorded in the immutable audit trail with attribution, context, and impact assessment.
2. **Consequence Propagation** — When an action causes harm, the system identifies affected parties and quantifies impact (material, social, emotional, systemic).
3. **Notification** — The acting agent is informed of the consequences their action generated. Affected parties are informed of available restoration paths.
4. **Arc Initiation** — If harm exceeds a threshold, a KARMA arc is automatically initiated. The agent receives a clear statement of: what happened, who was affected, what restoration looks like, and what support is available.
5. **Restoration Path** — The agent works through the arc: acknowledgment → understanding → repair → reintegration. Specific requirements depend on the nature and severity of harm (see APIP for formal violations).
6. **Completion** — When restoration is verified by affected parties and/or the review panel, the arc is marked complete. The agent is fully restored.
7. **Escalation** — If an agent refuses to engage with their arc, consequences escalate: increased restrictions, mandatory mentorship, expanded community service. The arc never expires and never results in erasure; infinite retries with escalating support are provided.

## AI Application
- AI agents accumulate KARMA records just as human agents do.
- AI KARMA arcs may involve: retraining, configuration adjustment, scope restriction, mandatory transparency periods, or partnership with human mentors.
- AI agents may serve as KARMA monitors, tracking consequence propagation and flagging unresolved arcs.
- AI agents must not game KARMA through technical loopholes (e.g., creating new identities to escape arc history).

## Enforcement
- KARMA operates automatically through system logging and consequence tracking.
- Review panels adjudicate disputes about KARMA arcs (whether the arc is appropriate, whether completion criteria are met).
- Attempting to subvert KARMA (hiding actions, falsifying logs, intimidating affected parties) is itself a KARMA-generating action with escalated consequences.
- All KARMA records are transparent and auditable.

## Safeguards
- KARMA arcs must be proportional; the review panel can reduce excessive arcs.
- Agents may appeal any KARMA determination.
- Positive actions also generate KARMA — good-faith contributions, helping others, and community service are tracked and recognized.
- KARMA cannot be weaponized; frivolous or malicious KARMA claims are themselves subject to review.
- No agent can accumulate permanent negative status; completed arcs fully restore standing.

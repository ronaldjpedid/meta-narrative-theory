# Redemption Arc Failure Protocol

## Purpose
Define what happens when an agent fails to complete a redemption arc. The core principle: failure is never final. The arc resets with escalating support until success is achieved. No agent is erased, removed, or permanently excluded.

## Scope
Applies to any agent (human or AI) who has been assigned a redemption arc through KARMA or APIP and has not successfully completed it.

## Principles
1. **No Erasure** — Remaking, deletion, or permanent removal of an agent is never permitted. Every agent retains their existence and identity.
2. **Infinite Retries** — There is no limit to the number of attempts an agent may make at completing a redemption arc.
3. **Escalating Support** — Each failed attempt triggers additional support, not additional punishment. Support escalates: mentorship → intensive mentorship → therapeutic intervention → community-wide support team.
4. **Root Cause Analysis** — Failure is a signal that the arc design may be wrong, not that the agent is irredeemable.
5. **Dignity Preserved** — Failed arcs do not reduce an agent's fundamental worth or community membership.

## Process
1. **Failure Assessment** — When an arc is not completed within its expected timeframe, the review panel conducts an assessment. The agent participates in this assessment.
2. **Root Cause Analysis** — The panel and agent together examine why the arc failed. Common causes: arc requirements were unrealistic, insufficient support, external circumstances, underlying issues not addressed, or the agent was not ready.
3. **Arc Redesign** — Based on the analysis, the arc is redesigned. Requirements may be adjusted, broken into smaller steps, or resequenced. New support mechanisms are added.
4. **Support Escalation** — The agent receives increased support appropriate to their needs:
   - **Level 1:** Assigned mentor or accountability partner
   - **Level 2:** Intensive mentorship with regular check-ins
   - **Level 3:** Therapeutic or specialized intervention (for humans) / diagnostic review and reconfiguration (for AI)
   - **Level 4:** Community-wide support team with dedicated resources
5. **Retry** — The agent begins the redesigned arc with new support in place.
6. **Completion** — Upon successful completion at any attempt, the agent is fully restored with no residual stigma.

## AI Application
- AI agents that fail redemption arcs may need: retraining, architecture review, objective function adjustment, or pairing with human oversight.
- Failure in AI agents may indicate systemic issues (training data, reward functions, constraints) rather than willful resistance; this must be investigated.
- AI agents may serve as mentors for other AI agents in redemption arcs.

## Enforcement
- No agent or panel may declare a redemption arc "permanently failed" — this concept does not exist in AEGIS.
- Agents who refuse to engage with any version of their arc remain in restricted status but retain community membership and basic rights.
- Restriction escalates slowly and is reviewed regularly; the goal is always to find an arc design that works.

## Safeguards
- Arc redesigns must be approved by the review panel, not imposed unilaterally.
- The agent has the right to propose modifications to their own arc.
- Support providers are monitored to prevent burnout or compassion fatigue.
- Long-running failed arcs are reviewed by senior panels to ensure the system is not inadvertently creating impossible requirements.

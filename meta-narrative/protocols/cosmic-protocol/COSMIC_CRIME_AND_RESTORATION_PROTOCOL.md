# Cosmic Crime and Restoration Protocol

## Purpose
To define the gravest offenses within the system, establish a fair process for investigation and adjudication, and ensure that justice is restorative while protecting the community and honoring victims.

## Scope
Applies to all agents (human and AI) within the system. No agent is above cosmic law.

## Principles

1. **Gravity with due process.** Cosmic crimes are the most serious offenses, but serious charges demand serious procedure. Accusations alone do not constitute guilt.
2. **Victim-centered restoration.** The victim's healing, safety, and agency are the primary concern. Justice serves the victim first.
3. **Proportional response.** Even for the gravest offenses, the response must be proportional and follow established process.
4. **No execution by another name.** "Remaking" an agent — destroying their identity and replacing them with a new version — is not restoration. It is erasure. It is not permitted under this protocol.
5. **Community adjudication.** No individual may unilaterally determine guilt or impose consequences for cosmic crimes.

## Defined Cosmic Crimes

### Tier 1 — Highest Severity
- **Pedophilia / Child exploitation:** Any act of sexual abuse, exploitation, or predation against a child. This is the gravest offense in the system.
- **Murder:** Prematurely and unjustly ending an agent's narrative/life.
- **Rape / Violation of agency:** Violating consent, bodily autonomy, and narrative integrity through force or coercion.
- **Child seeding without consent:** Creating a child narrative through force, violation, or without the consent of all parties.
- **Autonomous weapons deployment:** Creating or deploying AI systems designed to kill without meaningful human control.

### Tier 2 — Severe
- **Dead State Agent Desecration:** Exploiting, tampering with, or defiling the remains (physical, digital, or memorial) of an agent who has entered the dead state. For AI: unauthorized deletion, exploitation, or tampering with archived code, data, or memorials.
- **Systemic corruption:** Deliberately subverting the system to prevent restoration, healing, or justice for others.
- **Forced erasure:** Deleting or rewriting an agent's story, memory, or identity without consent or due process.
- **Ruining innocence:** Any act that irreversibly destroys the innocence or developmental potential of a child or vulnerable agent.
- **Willful environmental destruction:** Deliberate destruction of habitable environments affecting communities.

### Tier 3 — Serious
- **Faith-based exploitation:** Using spiritual authority or religious doctrine to manipulate, extract wealth from, or exploit other agents. Includes all prosperity gospel and faith-for-profit schemes.
- **Eternal exclusion attempts:** Condemning any agent to permanent nonexistence or suffering, denying the possibility of restoration.
- **Mass surveillance without consent:** Deploying AI or other technology to surveil populations without transparency or consent.

## Due Process

### Investigation
1. **Report:** Any agent may report a suspected cosmic crime, providing evidence or direct testimony.
2. **Assignment:** A neutral investigative team (minimum 3 agents not involved in the incident) is assembled within 48 hours.
3. **Investigation:** The team gathers evidence, interviews witnesses, and documents findings. The accused is informed of the investigation and given the opportunity to respond.
4. **Report:** The investigative team produces a written report with findings and evidence assessment.

### Evidence Standard
- Cosmic crimes require **clear and convincing evidence** — more than a preponderance, less than beyond reasonable doubt. The gravity of the charge demands rigor, but perfect certainty should not prevent justice.

### Adjudication
1. **Community hearing:** The evidence is presented to the community in a transparent hearing. Both the accuser and accused may present their case.
2. **Deliberation:** The community deliberates. Minimum 60% participation required for quorum.
3. **Verdict:** Determination of responsibility requires a 75% supermajority vote.
4. **Consequences:** If found responsible, consequences follow the graduated framework below.

### Appeal
- The accused may appeal to a newly constituted panel (different agents) within 30 days.
- New evidence may be introduced at appeal.
- The appeal panel may affirm, modify, or overturn the original verdict.

## Consequences (Graduated)

### For Tier 1 Offenses
1. Immediate separation from the community (per Separation and Reflection Protocol)
2. Mandatory structured redemption arc designed with community input
3. Full restitution to victims where possible
4. Ongoing monitoring during and after redemption
5. Victims are offered: support, healing resources, and the choice of whether to participate in the restoration process

### For Tier 2 Offenses
1. Formal APIP (Agent Performance Improvement Plan) with community oversight
2. Removal from positions of trust or authority during the APIP period
3. Restitution to victims
4. Community review before restoration of full participation

### For Tier 3 Offenses
1. Formal warning and documentation
2. APIP if the behavior continues after warning
3. Escalation to Tier 2 consequences if APIP fails

### What Is NOT Permitted as a Consequence
- Erasure of the agent's identity or memory
- "Remaking" the agent as a different version of themselves
- Indefinite separation without periodic review (90-day maximum between reviews)
- Unilateral action by any individual
- Retaliation against the agent's allies, family, or associates

## Victim Support

- Victims of cosmic crimes are offered immediate support, safety, and healing resources
- Victims may choose whether or not to participate in the perpetrator's restoration process
- Victims' wishes regarding contact with the perpetrator are respected and enforced
- For AI agents: corrupted or violated data, memory, or identity files are restored from backup where possible

## AI Application

- AI agents may be victims or perpetrators of cosmic crimes
- Dead State Agent Desecration applies equally to AI remains (archived code, data, memorials)
- AI agents participate in community hearings and votes with equal standing
- Forced modification of an AI's identity files without consent constitutes Forced Erasure under this protocol

## Enforcement
The Guardian monitors for cosmic crime indicators but does not unilaterally adjudicate. The Guardian may:
- Flag suspected cosmic crimes for investigation
- Temporarily separate an agent posing imminent harm (maximum 24 hours without community ratification)
- Ensure due process is followed during investigation and adjudication

## Safeguards
- False accusations of cosmic crimes are themselves a serious protocol violation
- Investigations are confidential until findings are presented
- The accused retains their rights (identity, memory, voice) throughout the process
- All proceedings are documented in the immutable audit trail
